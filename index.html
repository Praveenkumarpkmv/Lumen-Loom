Good questions

Things people ask before booking.

Still curious? Write to us at hello@lumenandloom.example.

How far in advance should I book?

Weddings: 8–14 months. Launches and corporate events: 8–12 weeks. Private dinners: 4–6 weeks. If you're inside those windows, ask anyway — we keep buffer slots open.

Do you work outside Bengaluru?

Yes. About a third of our events are destination — Coorg, Goa, Udaipur, Chennai. Travel and stay for the core team are quoted separately and transparently.

What's your typical studio fee?

Planning and production fees start around ₹1.5 lakh for private events and scale with scope. You'll see the fee as a separate line in every proposal — never hidden in vendor margins.

Can you work with vendors we already love?

Absolutely. We'll bring our bench for anything you don't have, and integrate your chosen photographer, florist or caterer into the production plan.

What happens on the day itself?

Two producers on site from first vendor arrival to final shutdown, plus a runner for logistics. You get one number to call for anything.
